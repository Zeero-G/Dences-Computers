# Dences Computers & Mobile

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-approved Next.js, React and TypeScript. Frontend first; backend, PostgreSQL and ERP operations are later phases. This phase exports a static, interactive site.

## Users

Customers shopping for new/used phones, computers and accessories in LKR on phones and PCs. Staff, owner and technician will use a later protected ERP.

## Product Purpose

One business with two shops. Let customers browse a shared catalogue, choose items, request a quotation, contact the shop, and eventually submit payment evidence for staff review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This phase has sample products, local cart/saved items, client-side filtering, product pages and request previews. It does not send orders, upload receipts, verify payments, reserve stock, or access production business data. No payment gateway. Full payment before goods leave is a later business rule. Offline billing belongs to the later shop system.

## Brand Commitments

Dences Computers & Mobile. User-supplied angular white logo. Adapt the user-approved monochrome Figma ecommerce kit to electronics and responsive desktop use. Smooth, restrained spring interactions.

## Evidence on Hand

The actual Figma catalogue, product and checkout screens have been read through MCP. The original logo is at /home/malan/Pictures/dences_logo.jpg. No real product catalogue, prices, addresses, telephone numbers, bank details, warranty policies, delivery terms, or customer reviews have been supplied. Demo values must be labelled.

## Product Principles

Keep availability and payment status honest. Make quotation and contact options easy to find. Protect private evidence when a backend exists. Keep separate shop inventory behind one catalogue. Use accessible controls and reduced-motion support.

## Open Decisions

Customer accounts, actual contact channels, delivery charges/zones and the production catalogue are still unconfirmed. Guest forms are a frontend demonstration, not a settled authentication policy.

## Repository Layout

The implemented app and dependency lockfile live in frontend/. Backend module and shared-contract folders are reserved for the later connected phase. Root DESIGN.md remains canonical. Architecture and current/planned diagrams are in docs/architecture/ and docs/diagrams/.
